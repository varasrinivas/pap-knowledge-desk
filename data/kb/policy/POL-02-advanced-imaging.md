# Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET)

This policy sets out what an office submits with a prior-authorization request for MRI, CT or PET under the IMAGING service type. It covers the documents, the conservative-therapy history and how recent the clinical notes must be. Questions about whether the imaging is medically necessary go to the Clinical Policy Desk.

## What must an advanced imaging request include?
An IMAGING request for MRI, CT or PET needs four things before it is complete. First, current clinical notes that describe the reason for the study, signed and dated by the ordering provider. Second, the conservative-therapy history when the study is for a musculoskeletal or pain complaint. Third, the report of any earlier imaging of the same body area. Fourth, a diagnosis code that matches the requested study. Missing items lead to a pend: PI-DOC-001 for missing notes, PI-DOC-003 for a missing imaging report, PI-DOC-004 for missing therapy history and PI-DOC-007 for a mismatched diagnosis code.

## What does the conservative-therapy history need to show?
The conservative-therapy history for an imaging request lists every therapy the member tried before imaging was ordered. For each therapy, give the type (for example physical therapy, medication or home exercise), the start and end dates, the duration and the outcome. A history that names a therapy without dates or outcome is treated as missing and the request is pended with PI-DOC-004. If the request is decided without it, the denial code is CR-ND-001, which is appealable.

## How recent must the clinical notes be for imaging?
Clinical notes supporting an MRI, CT or PET request must be dated within 60 days of submission. Notes older than 60 days lead to a pend with PI-DOC-002. If the request is decided on old notes, the denial code is CR-ND-002, which is appealable. The 60-day rule applies to the notes, not to earlier imaging reports, which may be older and are still useful as history.

## Should earlier imaging reports be uploaded?
Yes. When the member has had earlier imaging of the same body area, the office uploads the radiologist's report on step **5 Documents** of the wizard. The report is enough; image files themselves are not needed. A request that refers to earlier imaging without the report is pended with PI-DOC-003. Upload reports as PDF, JPG, PNG, TIFF or DOCX, at most 25 MB per file. ZIP files are not accepted.

## Which units and dates should an imaging request show?
An imaging request should show one unit per study and a single planned date of service or a short date range. When a request asks for both an MRI and a CT, enter them as separate service lines. Unclear units or dates lead to a pend with PI-DOC-006. The servicing provider's NPI must be valid, or the request is pended with PI-DOC-010.
