# Home health and durable medical equipment

This policy sets out what an office submits for home health services and durable medical equipment (DME) under the HOME_HEALTH_DME service type. It covers the face-to-face encounter note and how to request rental or purchase. Questions about whether a service or item meets clinical criteria go to the Clinical Policy Desk.

## What must a home health or DME request include?
A HOME_HEALTH_DME request needs the ordering provider's signed order, clinical notes dated within 60 days of submission, a face-to-face encounter note, a diagnosis code that matches the service or item, and the servicing provider's valid NPI. The servicing provider is the home health agency or the DME supplier. A missing face-to-face note is pended with PI-DOC-008, an unsigned order with PI-DOC-005, and a missing or invalid NPI with PI-DOC-010.

## What is a face-to-face encounter note?
The face-to-face encounter note documents a visit in which the ordering provider, or an allowed practitioner working with them, saw the member and assessed the need for the home health service or equipment. The note must be signed and dated, must name the service or item being ordered, and must fall within the 60-day window for clinical notes. A visit summary that does not mention the ordered service or item does not clear PI-DOC-008.

## How do I request DME as a rental or a purchase?
On step **2 Service** of the wizard, choose rental or purchase for each DME item. A rental is requested in monthly units with a start and end date. A purchase is requested as one unit with a single date of service. When the units do not match the choice, for example a purchase with twelve units, the request is pended with PI-DOC-006. Whether an item should be rented or bought is decided by the plan's benefit rules, not by the service desk.

## How are home health visits requested?
Home health services are requested as a number of visits per discipline, such as skilled nursing or therapy, over a date range. Enter each discipline as a separate service line. The number of visits and the date range must agree with the signed order. Unclear visit counts or dates lead to a pend with PI-DOC-006. Additional visits after approval are requested through a new request that references the PA number, as described in POL-15.

## How does an office clear a home health or DME pend?
A pended HOME_HEALTH_DME request is cleared by uploading exactly what the pend code names through the **Respond to Pend** panel on the request detail screen. The request then returns to Pending Clinical Review and the decision clock resumes. A pend with no response in 14 calendar days closes as Withdrawn. Do not email documents; upload them in the portal. Uploaded files appear on the **Documents** tab within 2 minutes.
