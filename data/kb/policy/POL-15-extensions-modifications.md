# Extensions and modifications

This policy explains how an office adds units or extends dates on an approved authorization, and how to change dates of service on a request that has not been decided yet. Approved authorizations are not edited in place.

## How do I add units to an approved authorization?
To add units to an approved authorization, submit a new request through **New request** that references the existing PA number, for example `PA-10482`. Enter the PA number in the clinical step, request only the additional units, and upload current clinical notes that support them. The original authorization stays valid for what it already covers. The new request is reviewed on its own 72-hour STANDARD or 24-hour URGENT turnaround.

## How do I extend the dates on an approved authorization?
To extend the dates of service on an approved authorization, submit a new request that references the existing PA number and states the new end date. Submit it before the current authorization ends, so there is no gap in coverage. Include clinical notes dated within 60 days of submission. An extension request is reviewed like any other request and may be pended or denied.

## Why reference the PA number on an extension request?
Referencing the PA number tells the reviewer that the request extends an existing authorization rather than repeating it. A new request for the same service and dates that does not reference the PA may be denied with CR-ND-006, "Duplicate request (an approved authorization already exists)". CR-ND-006 is not appealable; the next step is to use the existing PA, or to resubmit as an extension that references it.

## How do I change the dates of service before a decision?
Changing dates of service before a decision depends on the request status. A request in Draft can be edited freely; open it from the worklist, correct the dates, and select **Save draft** or **Submit**. After submission, send the corrected dates to the reviewer on the **Messages** tab. If the reviewer pends the request with PI-DOC-006, "Units or dates of service unclear", upload the corrected dates through **Respond to Pend**.

## Can the service desk change an authorization for me?
No. The service desk and the assistant cannot change, extend or override an authorization or a decision. They can explain how to submit an extension request and help with portal problems that block it, such as a failed upload. Questions about whether more units are clinically appropriate go to the Clinical Policy Desk. The status of an extension request is shown on its own **Timeline** tab.
