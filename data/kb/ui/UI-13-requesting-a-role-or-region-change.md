# Requesting a role or region change

Use this when you need a different role (for example office manager instead of nurse), access to another region, or a change of approver group. Access changes are requested from **My Access** and approved by a manager or regional director; they are not made by the Service Desk on request.

## Procedure: Check your current access
1. Sign in and open **My Access**.
2. Read **Your role**, for example PROVIDER_NURSE or UM_NURSE.
3. Read **Your regions**: `SOUTH_CENTRAL`, `NORTHEAST` or `WEST`.
4. Compare these with what you need for the task you are trying to do.

**What you should see:** Your role and regions exactly as the portal holds them. If they are already right, the problem is something else, such as a lapsed delegation (UI-12).

## Procedure: Request a role or region change
1. On **My Access**, click **Request a change**. The access catalog form opens.
2. Choose what you want to change: role, region or approver group.
3. Select the new value, for example the additional region.
4. Write the business reason in one or two sentences (for example "Covering Boston office reviews from 1 November").
5. Check the approver shown on the form: your manager or your regional director.
6. Submit the form and note the request number.

**What you should see:** A confirmation that the request was sent to your approver. After approval, the change is provisioned within one business day. Sign out and sign in again to see the new values on **My Access**.

## If you use the support assistant instead
- You can also ask for an access change through **Help** > **Contact support**. The assistant drafts the same access catalog request and shows it to you.
- The assistant always asks you to confirm before it submits. Check the role, region and reason before you say yes.

## Things to know
- Nothing changes until your approver approves. Provisioning then takes one business day.
- Delegation to submit for an office is not a role change. If a nurse needs to submit for the office, the office manager adds a delegation (UI-12).
- UM nurses see requests for their assigned region only. A region change is the way to see another region's queue.

## Common errors on this screen
- **"I submitted the request yesterday and nothing has changed"**: It may still be waiting for approval. Ask your approver; provisioning takes one business day after approval.
- **"It was approved but My Access still shows the old role"**: Your session started before the change. Sign out and sign in again.
- **"I picked the wrong approver"**: The approver is set by your role and region. If it is wrong, raise it through **Help** > **Contact support**.
- **"I need to submit for my office, so I asked for office manager"**: You probably need a delegation, not a role change. Ask your office manager (UI-12).
