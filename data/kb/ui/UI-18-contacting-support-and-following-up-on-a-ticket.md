# Contacting support and following up on a ticket

Use this when you need help with the portal: a locked account, a screen that is not working, a request in the wrong queue, or a how-to question. Support starts from **Help** > **Contact support**, which opens the support assistant.

## Procedure: Contact support from the portal
1. Click **Help**.
2. Click **Contact support**. The support assistant opens.
3. Describe the problem in a sentence or two: what you were doing, the screen, the button and the exact error text.
4. If it concerns a request, give the `IR-` number only. Do not type member names, dates of birth or clinical details.
5. Read the assistant's answer. Many questions are answered straight away from the portal guides.
6. If a ticket is needed, the assistant creates it and gives you the incident number (`INC00NNNNN`). Write it down.

**What you should see:** Either an answer to your question, or an `INC00NNNNN` number for your ticket.

## Procedure: Follow up on a ticket
1. Click **Help** > **Your tickets**.
2. Find the ticket by its `INC00NNNNN` number and open it.
3. Read the status and the latest updates from the Service Desk.
4. To add information, add a comment to the same ticket. Do not open a new one for the same problem.
5. When you contact support again about it, quote the `INC00NNNNN` number first.

**What you should see:** The ticket's history, including any work notes and the date of the last update.

## What support can and cannot do
- The Service Desk can unlock accounts, investigate defects, fix data issues and route requests to the right queue.
- It cannot change, speed up or override a decision, and cannot answer clinical-criteria questions; those go to the Clinical Policy Desk.
- Appeals are not tickets. File them with **Appeal this decision** (UI-10); questions about an appeal go to the Appeals Unit.

## If there is a known outage
- When many users report the same problem, it is linked to one major incident. The assistant tells you its number and when the next update is due; for a P1 outage, updates come every 60 minutes.
- Your report is added to the existing ticket as a note instead of creating a new one.

## Common errors on this screen
- **"I opened a ticket and got told it's a duplicate"**: Someone already reported the same issue. Follow that ticket's number on **Your tickets** for updates.
- **"I can't find my ticket"**: Open **Your tickets** and search by the `INC00NNNNN` number from the assistant.
- **"I'm locked out and can't get to Help"**: Use the **Help** link on the **Sign in** screen. Lockouts are not self-service (UI-02).
- **"Support won't change our denial"**: Correct; nobody at the Service Desk can. Use **Appeal this decision** or, where offered, **Request peer-to-peer**.
